Our School's Computer Laboratory

Our school's computer laboratory was set up to give every student hands-on access to modern technology. Equipped with updated units and a stable internet connection, it allows learners to practice typing, research, and complete multimedia projects with ease. Teachers use the lab not only for computer subjects but also for integrating digital tools into other lessons, helping students build skills they will carry beyond the classroom. The lab is open during free periods, giving students extra time to finish schoolwork or explore new programs under the guidance of the school's IT staff.
