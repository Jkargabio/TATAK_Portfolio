Whispers of the Morning

The sun climbs slow above the hill,
It paints the sky in gold and still,
The birds awake with cheerful sound,
And dew drops sparkle on the ground.
The wind moves softly through the trees,
It hums a tune to greet the breeze,
The river winds its gentle way,
Reflecting light of early day.
And as the morning fully wakes,
New hope arrives with all it takes,
A quiet world begins to hum,
Another day has just begun.
