Every TATAK has a story.

Love what someone’s wearing? Check the neck tag, type the name below, and find the brand, where it’s from, and where to shop it.

Small brands are hard to find. We fix that.

Tatak is a free directory of clothing brands. Spot a shirt you like, read the name on the tag, and we’ll show you who made it and where to shop it.

What we do

We gather brands in one place, with their website, socials, and shops. Every link goes to the brand itself.

What we don’t do

We don’t sell clothes or take payments. You buy straight from the brand, so there are no middlemen and no extra fees.

Who it’s for

Shoppers who want to find a brand, and new makers who want to be found.

Mission

Help people find the clothing brands behind the tags, and help small brands get found.

Vision

One place where every local clothing brand can be discovered.

Our services

Tatak helps shoppers find brands, and helps brands get found.

For shoppers

  1. Check the tag. Find the brand name on the neck tag.
  2. Type it in. We’ll find it even if you misspell it a little.
  3. Go to the brand. Open its page and follow the links to its shop or socials.

For brand owners

  1. List your brand. Fill in a short form.
  2. Get checked. We review every brand to keep out fakes and copies.
  3. Get found. Show up in search, and in the New and Brand of the day spots.

Our history

Tatak started with a simple problem: you like a shirt, but you can’t find who made it.

  • The idea. Small clothing brands are hard to find, so we planned one place to search for them by the name on the neck tag.
  • The first website. We built Tatak with brand search, brand pages, and a daily Brand of the day.
  • Presenting at school. We’re showing the first version to our class and collecting feedback.
  • Next. A database and brand sign-ups, so real brands can list themselves after a review.

Try Tatak yourself

Each exercise below takes you to a Tatak feature and sets it up for you.

  1. Find a brand by its name. Search “Salkot” with a spelling mistake. Tatak still finds Salakot Supply.
